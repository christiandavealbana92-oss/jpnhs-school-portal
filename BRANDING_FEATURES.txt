School Portal Branding Update

Administrator > School > School Branding:
- Upload/remove school logo.
- Add/edit/remove partner organizations with partner logos.
- Branding is saved under schools/{schoolId}/logo and schools/{schoolId}/partners.

All school portals load the branding from the active school's Firebase record.
School logo and partner logos/names appear in portal headers and generated PDF reports.
Registrar and Teacher Excel exports use ExcelJS and embed the school/partner logos.
Administrator complete backup Excel also includes a Branding sheet with embedded logos.

Images are resized/compressed in the browser before being stored in Realtime Database to keep the stored data smaller.
